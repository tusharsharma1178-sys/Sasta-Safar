🚗 Sasta Safar

Sasta Safar is a peer-to-peer travel sharing platform that helps people find affordable travel options by connecting travelers who are already going to the same destination.

📌 Problem Statement

Many people, especially students and working professionals, face high travel costs when traveling between cities. At the same time, many vehicles and travel seats have unused space.

Sasta Safar aims to connect these two groups and make inter-city travel more affordable and convenient.

💡 Our Solution

A person who is already traveling from one city to another can list their available seat or space on Sasta Safar.

Other users traveling on the same route can find the trip and contact the traveler.

This creates a win-win situation:

- Travelers get affordable travel options.
- Vehicle owners can share travel costs or earn from unused space.
- Empty seats/space can be utilized.

✨ Key Features

- 🔍 Search trips by source and destination
- 🚗 Share available seats in a vehicle
- 👥 Connect travelers going on similar routes
- 💰 Affordable cost-sharing
- 🤝 Negotiation between users
- 📦 Option for goods/package delivery
- 📱 Simple and user-friendly interface
- 🔐 User registration and login

- Prayagraj → Kanpur

🔄 How It Works

1. User creates an account.
2. Traveler posts a trip with source, destination, date and available seats.
3. Other users search for available trips.
4. Users find a suitable trip.
5. They connect with the traveler.
6. Travel cost is shared or negotiated.
7. Both users complete their journey.
